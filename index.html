About Elevate

More Than Coffee

Elevate Coffee Academy & Coffee Shop is a place where coffee passion meets professional skills.

We provide practical barista training for beginners, intermediate learners and future coffee professionals.

At the same time, our coffee shop gives customers quality coffee, fresh drinks and a welcoming experience.

Our Vision

To help people turn their passion for coffee into professional skills and opportunities.
